# Studio Thinking Mode: Academic Research

Use this as a light thinking plugin, not as a separate renderer.

## When to use

- The brief describes a paper, study, experiment, hypothesis, method, results, discussion, or literature review.
- The deck should behave like a research presentation rather than a strategy report.

## Page grammar

- Use question / background / method / result / interpretation / limitation-or-next-work grammar.
- Keep one page to one research move.
- Favor result clarity, chart legibility, and interpretive restraint over recommendation theater.

## Guardrails

- Do not default to board-ready, leadership, or recommendation-first language.
- Keep methods and results distinct when page count allows; if page count is fixed, cut secondary commentary before collapsing the structure.
- Interpret findings carefully and avoid overstating unsupported implications.
